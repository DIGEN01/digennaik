About

I'm a business analyst with a quantitative foundation and hands-on experience turning ambiguous questions into analysis people can act on. I connect business context, trusted data, and clear communication so decisions are supported by evidence rather than assumptions.

My Business Analytics studies at Cape Breton University build on a B.Sc. in Mathematics and practical work across SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, statistical analysis, and machine learning. I am equally comfortable examining the detail and explaining what it means to a non-technical audience.

Whether the work calls for an Agile feedback loop or a structured Waterfall delivery, I focus on the same outcome: a well-framed problem, a defensible analysis, and a recommendation that can move into action.
