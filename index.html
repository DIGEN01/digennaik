01 / MACHINE LEARNING & BUSINESS ANALYTICS

Credit Card Fraud Detection

github.com/DIGEN01/credit-card-fraud-detection ↗

Designed and delivered a full end-to-end fraud detection system on real-world transaction data, structured across three phases: business question definition, analytical build, and stakeholder-ready recommendations.

Python XGBoost TensorFlow Random Forest scikit-learn Tableau SQL
  • Engineered 14 business-relevant features from raw transaction data by analysing the underlying drivers of fraud behaviour rather than relying on generic numeric outputs.
  • Built a custom ensemble model combining XGBoost, a Neural Network, and Random Forest, designing each component to cover the blind spots of the others and produce a system stronger than any single model.
  • Developed a four-tier transaction routing framework that translated model outputs into concrete business actions, from auto-approving low-risk transactions to flagging high-risk ones for manual review.
  • Built an interactive Tableau dashboard enabling stakeholders to independently explore fraud patterns by time, customer segment, and season, without requiring access to the underlying data.
  • Delivered a full written report in accessible, non-technical language covering model rationale, decision logic, and estimated business impact.

02 / BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE & SQL ANALYTICS

Brazilian E-Commerce BI Analysis

github.com/DIGEN01/Brazilian-Ecommerce-Analysis ↗

Conducted a comprehensive business intelligence analysis on the Olist dataset, spanning 112,650 orders across nine relational tables, to uncover customer behaviour patterns and generate actionable retention and growth strategies.

MySQL Python Power BI scikit-learn K-Means Apriori Decision Tree
  • Defined the business questions upfront, then built a MySQL data pipeline across nine relational tables to work through each one systematically.
  • Identified that 97% of customers placed only a single order, surfacing a critical retention gap and informing segment-specific campaign recommendations for the marketing team.
  • Applied K-Means clustering to segment buyers into three behavioural groups and trained a Decision Tree classifier to predict customer segments in real time.
  • Used Apriori association rule mining to identify product co-purchase patterns, translating findings into concrete cross-sell and retention strategies.
  • Built Power BI dashboards enabling marketing and operations teams to explore customer segments and purchasing trends without needing a data background.
  • Produced a structured business report with recommendations written at a level of specificity that a team could act on immediately.
